The enabled backend covers identity/status, profile and key/Fn maps, macros, OS
options, main and side lighting, pictures, switch selection, magnetic reads and
actuation/mode/snap workflows, timed USB calibration, and schema 7 backup and
restore. USB is the supported transport; hardware behavior has not been
verified on a physical HE108.

The catalog declares six switch names (`高特`, `磁玉`, `磁玉pro`, `磁玉gaming`,
`天王`, `万磁王`), corresponding to switch codes 0–5. HE108 sleep limits are
60–3600 seconds for 24G and Bluetooth, with deep Bluetooth limited to
600–3600 seconds; the deep-dongle word is retained but not exposed as a public
control. Travel is 0.1–3.3 mm in 0.005 mm steps, with a default of 2 mm. Active
firmware precision still limits which values can be represented on the wire.
These are catalog/UI limits, not hardware verification.

Recovery preserves raw inactive fields, macros, pictures, lighting, Fn layers,
and model-specific settings subject to the shared schema and exact identity and
firmware-version checks. No recoil-control, additional display, or
firmware-management capability is established by this catalog entry.
